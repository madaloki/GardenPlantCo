About Us

Garden Plant Co. has been helping neighbors grow healthier, happier gardens since 2015. What started as a small family plant stand has grown into a full-service shop offering everything from seasonal flowers and vegetable starters to expert lawn and garden care. We believe every yard, big or small, deserves a little green magic. Our friendly staff loves sharing tips with first-time gardeners and seasoned green thumbs alike. Whether you're planting your first tomato or redesigning your backyard, we're here to help it thrive. Stop by, say hello, and let's grow something great together.
